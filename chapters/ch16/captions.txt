He stares at red and green numbers bleeding down
The weekend high evaporates in real-time market death

Bitcoin tanked eighteen hours ago and his fortune shrinks
Eight weeks of runway left if they stop eating

Cloud servers overdue. Lab rent due Monday. Taxes screaming
The life-changing payout was just a life-preserver all along

He looks at her photo—Erica laughing in Berlin
She deserves stability. She deserves a man who wins

Lena moves through the world with dangerous, effortless grace
She's not just a scout. She's his lifeline now

He opens encrypted mail with trembling fingers hovering
One message away from crossing into territory unmarked

Traditional VC is too slow. The Whale waits
He needs the second recovery. He needs to prove himself

His reflection stares back from the trading screen glow
The rush returns—he doesn't think about the shadows anymore

One click and the message vanishes into encrypted darkness
Sent. No turning back from what he just started

He leans back in his office chair, breathing shallow
The hunt begins again and fear never even shows up
