Her heels crack against convention floor
Three missed calls. One last chance to survive.

Paul sits alone behind a laminate desk
His name on a roster: everything depends on her.

She drops an encrypted tablet down hard
"I saw your keynote. Prove it works."

Forty-eight bits of a shattered private key
Two hundred bits of pure digital chaos remain.

Paul's heartbeat matches the quantum probability cloud
Weeks of cooling time they simply don't have.

She leans closer, voice dropping ice cold
"Ten percent of the wallet. Or nothing at all."

His smartwatch screams a low balance alert
Her boss waits for results, not excuses.

Paul sees the IBM team circling nearby
One handshake decides if he stays afloat.

She extends her hand across the desk
He takes it. The leap into dark begins.

"Bring me the drive," he whispers back
Everything changes in the next forty-eight hours.
