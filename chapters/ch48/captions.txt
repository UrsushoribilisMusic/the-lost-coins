The mill falls silent. Emergency lights paint everything blue and red.
Paul leans on Erica. Every step costs him.

The paramedic asks the routine questions. Standard screening.
Erica's eyes find Paul. She breathes deep.

"I'm pregnant. Five weeks."
The ambulance freezes. Paul sits up—pain forgotten.

His jaw drops. His exhausted eyes go wide.
"We're going to be... parents?"

She takes his hand. That triumphant smile breaks through.
"I was waiting for the right moment."

Chen laughs from the doorway, bruised but grinning.
"The world needs more Pandas, my friend."

The medic presses Paul's ribs. He winces.
"Nothing's broken, Dad. You'll hold that newborn."

Paul looks at the mill. The chaos. The greed.
It all feels like a nightmare now.

Erica leans down. Kisses his forehead gently.
"We're going home. Really home."

The ancient Sequoias stand witness above them all.
New life blooms from the ashes of fire.

Survivors plan futures that have nothing to do with death.
