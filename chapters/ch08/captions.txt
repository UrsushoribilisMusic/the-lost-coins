A man in obsidian silence watches screens flicker
His patience wears thin like a blade

The voice crackles—raspy, dangerous, thrilled with secrets
"I found what everyone's been hunting for"

Frozen flesh. Liquid nitrogen. Two hundred sleeping gods
One brain holds billions nobody can touch

"His wallet never moved. Virgin coins. Untouched."
The dead man's fortune sleeps in Arizona

Ice crystals should destroy everything—memories, keys, hope
But what if the science just evolved?

A neuroscientist's face flashes across encrypted lines
She can read what's locked inside ice

"Adapt the tech," the voice demands, hungry
"Plug into his frozen mind or I'll find someone else"

The obsidian desk goes silent. The choice hardens.
One impossible heist. One frozen billionaire. One shot.
