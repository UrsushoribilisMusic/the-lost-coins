She checks into the Fairmont like she owns it
Five-star elegance masking five years of lies

The phone vibrates. His voice cuts through silence
"I'm coming to California. Personal. Midnight arrival."

Her hand trembles against the mahogany desk
The dream of escape just caught fire

She spins around. Two men emerge from shadows
Inspector Pinto. FBI. She's already calculated the prison sentence

"We know about Macau. The cartels. Everything."
But they don't want her. They want him.

The choice crystallizes in one suffocating moment
Stay loyal, disappear. Run, become a target.

She looks at her phone. Still warm from his voice
One confession ends everything she's built

"He's coming," she whispers, the words escaping
"And I'm the only thing standing between you both"

Midnight in California. The warehouse in the woods
She just became the most dangerous person alive
