Midnight. Paul's still hunting something in the code.
Blue screens wash his face in electric dread.

The door creaks. Erica appears like a ghost.
Steam rises from her tea. She looks concerned.

"Any luck?" Her voice cracks with exhausted hope.
His fingers blur across the keyboard. He whispers back.

"They shattered the key into digital pieces. Scattered."
Erica's eyes widen. She doesn't understand yet.

"Shamir's Secret Sharing. Like horcruxes made of math."
One piece means nothing. Two might mean nothing.

Paul points at blocks of glowing encrypted data.
"The blockchain never forgets. It only knows numbers."

"So we're stuck?" She leans against his chair.
His eyes reflect scrolling text. Pure electric hunger.

"No. I find the threshold, I break this open."
Three segments glow on his monitor like treasure.

Her hand touches his shoulder. Soft. Electric. Brief.
"Don't chase ghosts all night," she whispers low.

She leaves. He's already gone into the hunt.
A hound catching blood-scent on the digital wind.
