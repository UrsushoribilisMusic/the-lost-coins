Inspector Pinto's fingers drum against his desk. The trail won't wait for morning.

He patches through to Interpol. Lyon bounces to Bern. Switzerland picks up.

Captain Meier's voice cuts through like ice. "We flagged that Bitcoin ten minutes ago."

Pinto leans forward. This wallet isn't random. It's a direct payment, not laundering.

Eighteen months chasing Mr. Z. The ghost never leaves a trail this clean.

Meier hesitates. Freeze the account or watch? One move loses the kingpin forever.

"Don't freeze it," Pinto commands. "He's paying someone in Zug. A middleman. A bridge."

If they move too fast, Z vanishes into fog. If they wait, they hunt him.

Meier agrees. Passive surveillance. KYC data incoming. Bank monitors activated within the hour.

Pinto's smile turns grim. "These people are professionals. One wrong move, everything burns."

"We're Switzerland," Meier replies coolly. "We specialize in being invisible."
