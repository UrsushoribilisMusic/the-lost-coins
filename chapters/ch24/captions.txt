Paul walks through Zurich Airport like he owns it
His laptop bag feels like a trophy now

Business class. Twelve hours of actual silverware ahead
He's never sat this close to the clouds

Lena moves through the lounge with practiced grace
Cream silk and a status card that opens doors

Erica watches her. Still skeptical. Still curious
The "maybe" from last night became a yes

Champagne touches their lips at 35,000 feet
"To transparency," Erica says. Lena doesn't flinch

The cabin lights dim. The game tightens
Lena's eyes track Erica across the aisle

Erica unbuckles and leans into the shadows
"You're watching me again." Not a complaint

Lena reaches toward her face, then stops
"You're looking for the signal." So is she

"Who are you really working for?" Erica whispers
The question cuts. But her eyes beg for trust

Lena tastes copper when she lies about the future
Ten rows back, someone photographs the back of her head

The Feds in California already have the manifest
She doesn't know. She only feels her fall
