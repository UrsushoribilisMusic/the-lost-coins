The lab falls away behind her
Lena's heart hammers like it might burst

She hides behind concrete, hands shaking steady
A voice answers before the first ring ends

"The girl isn't a mercenary," Lena whispers
"She's already doing it. She's the key"

Zheng's silence tastes like something predatory
"Make yourself indispensable. Become their best friend"

Erica's warm wink flashes through her mind
But orders turn that warmth into a trap

"I understand," Lena says into the dead phone
Her voice sounds like someone else's voice

The wallet refreshes with half a bitcoin
Blood money glows brighter than she expected

She stares at stolen numbers on screen
Enough to buy a year of lies

"The past is just a story," she whispers
But this story has teeth and it hungers

She closes her eyes against the guilt
Tomorrow she'll smile and start the seduction

The price of betrayal tastes like copper
And she's already swallowed it whole
